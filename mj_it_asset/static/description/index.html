IT Asset Management

Register IT equipment by serial number, hand it over to employees with a complete, auditable history, link every asset to its Purchase Order and let employees and IT vendors work from the portal.

Odoo 18.0 Community & Enterprise Backend + Portal LGPL-3

Why this module?

Odoo's Assets app is an accounting tool (depreciation) and does not answer the questions an IT team asks every day: who has this laptop, since when, who had it before, which PO did it come from, and what happens when an employee leaves? This module is a standalone IT asset register, not linked to Products or Accounting Assets, so it works the same on Community and Enterprise without any accounting set-up.

Key Features

  • Standalone IT asset register – automatic asset code (ITA/00001), category, brand, model, location, specification and photo.
  • Serial number tracking – mandatory and unique per company; search by serial everywhere.
  • Assign / Re-assign / Return with condition (New, Good, Fair, Damaged), notes and dates.
  • Complete assignment history – the previous holder is closed with a reason (Employee left, Transfer, Upgrade, Repair, Lost …); nothing is ever overwritten.
  • Employee leaving? – re-assign or return all their assets in one click from the employee form.
  • Editable, tracked history – corrections are allowed and every change is logged in the chatter.
  • Lifecycle states – Available, Assigned, In Repair, Lost / Stolen, Retired / Disposed.
  • Purchase Order link – link assets to a PO and PO line; vendor, date and cost are filled automatically.
  • Create assets from a confirmed PO – one line per ordered unit, just type the serial numbers.
  • Smart buttons on Employee and Purchase Order.
  • Customer portal (/my/it-assets) with search, filters, sorting and Group By.
  • Employees see the equipment assigned to them; IT vendors / partners (portal users) can fully manage assets.
  • Data integrity – one holder at a time, no overlapping periods, no duplicate serials.
  • Security – read-only users, managers, and deletion reserved to Settings administrators; multi-company ready.

1. IT Asset Register

All assets with their serial number, current holder, department, Purchase Order and status. Filter by status, warranty expiry, assets still held by employees who have left, and group by category, employee, department, PO or vendor.

2. Asset Form & Assignment History

Identification, current holder, purchase & warranty information, specification and the full hand-over history of the asset. Every action is posted in the chatter.

3. Assign & Re-assign

Hand an asset (or several at once) to an employee. If it is already held by someone, their assignment is closed automatically with the reason you choose – e.g. Employee Left / Resigned.

4. Return

Record the return date, reason and condition, and decide the next status (back to IT stock, In Repair, Lost, Retired). A damaged return suggests In Repair automatically.

5. Create IT Assets from a Purchase Order

A Create IT Assets button on confirmed Purchase Orders proposes one line per ordered unit that is not registered yet. Enter the serial numbers and the assets are created already linked to the PO, the PO line and the vendor, with the unit cost.

6. Employee Form

An IT Assets smart button and tab show what the employee currently holds and everything they held before. When someone leaves: Re-assign All to Another Employee or Return All to IT.

7. Assignment History

Every hand-over of every asset in one list: employee and department at the time of assignment, dates, days used, conditions and return reason.

Customer Portal

Employees linked to a portal user (Related User or Work Contact) see the assets assigned to them. Portal users flagged as IT Asset Portal Manager – e.g. your outsourced IT vendor – can add, edit, assign, re-assign and return assets, manage categories and create assets from Purchase Orders, without access to the Odoo backend.

Access Rights

GroupWhat they can do
IT Asset / UserView assets and assignment history (read-only), backend and portal.

Settings / AdministrationEverything above, plus delete (deletion is reserved to administrators).
IT Asset Portal Manager (portal users)Full asset management from the portal, limited to their companies.
Employees (portal or internal)See the assets currently assigned to them and their own history on the portal.

Getting Started

  1. Install IT Asset Management (depends on Employees, Purchase and Portal).
  2. Give your IT team the IT Asset / Manager access right.
  3. Create your categories in IT Assets › Configuration › Categories (Laptop, Monitor, Printer …).
  4. Register assets manually or with Create IT Assets on a confirmed Purchase Order.
  5. Use Assign, Re-assign and Return – the history is kept automatically.
  6. Optional: tick IT Asset Portal Manager on portal users who should manage assets from the portal.
